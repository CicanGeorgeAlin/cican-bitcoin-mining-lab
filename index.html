How to do it yourself

01
Choose a cryptocurrency and algorithm.
Start with an algorithm your device can actually run. RandomX is a useful CPU experiment.
02
Create your own wallet.
Never share your seed phrase or private keys. Use your own receiving address.
03
Install a miner for your device.
On Android, a terminal such as Termux can be used for compatible CPU miners.
04
Connect to a pool.
Use the pool's official documentation and enter your own wallet address.
05
Measure before you believe the numbers.
Record hashrate, accepted shares, temperature, runtime and electricity use.

Example: our RandomX test

Pixel 6 · 8 threads · RandomX · XMRPool TLS primo-arm-miner -a randomx -o stratum+ssl://xmrpool.eu:3333 -u YOUR_WALLET_ADDRESS -p x -t 8 Replace YOUR_WALLET_ADDRESS with your own wallet address. Never paste a seed phrase or private key.

How to improve the result

1. Test thread counts.
More threads do not always mean better efficiency. Compare performance at different settings.
2. Control heat.
Thermal throttling can reduce sustained hashrate. Cooling and airflow matter.
3. Measure electricity.
Battery readings are not the same as wall power. Use a USB-C power meter for proper input measurements.
4. Compare devices.
Hashrate alone is not enough. Compare hashrate per watt and estimated earnings per day.
5. Separate benchmark from real mining.
A local benchmark proves what hardware can calculate. Pool mining shows whether the complete setup actually works.
