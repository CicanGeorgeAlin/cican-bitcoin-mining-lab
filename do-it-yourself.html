CICAN / DO IT YOURSELF

Run your first experiment.

1. Prepare

Choose hardware you own and understand. Keep your device cool and plugged into a safe power source.

2. Wallet

Create a wallet using trusted software. Never put your seed phrase or private key into a miner, website, chat or random script.

Official Monero downloads →

3. Terminal

On Android, Termux provides a terminal environment. Use the official project source.

Termux on GitHub →

4. Miner

Our Pixel experiment used Primo ARM Miner with RandomX.

primo-arm-miner -a randomx -o stratum+ssl://xmrpool.eu:3333 -u YOUR_WALLET_ADDRESS -p x -t 8Use your own wallet address. This example is documentation of our experiment, not a guarantee of profitability.

5. Observe

Record hashrate, accepted shares, rejected shares, temperature and runtime. Stop if the device becomes excessively hot or unstable.

6. Measure electricity

Battery-current apps can help observe battery-side behavior, but they do not equal wall/input power. For proper energy measurement, use a suitable USB-C power meter.

7. Calculate

Return to the CICAN calculator and enter measured values. Label estimates as estimates.
