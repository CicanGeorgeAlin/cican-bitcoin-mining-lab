Understand the machine.

Mining is not magic. A miner repeatedly performs proof-of-work calculations. The network accepts valid work according to its consensus rules.

Hashrate

Hashrate is the number of hashing attempts a device can perform per unit of time. Higher is not automatically better: energy efficiency and hardware cost matter too.

Proof of Work

Proof of work makes a miner search for a result that satisfies a network target. Finding a valid result is probabilistic; verification is comparatively straightforward.

RandomX

Monero uses RandomX for proof of work. It is designed around general-purpose CPUs rather than specialized ASIC mining hardware.

Solo, pool and P2Pool

  • Solo: you work independently and may wait a long time for a block.
  • Pool: miners combine work and receive rewards according to the pool's rules.
  • P2Pool: a decentralized peer-to-peer mining pool.

Bitcoin vs our educational benchmark

A simple double-SHA-256 program can teach proof-of-work, but it is not Bitcoin network mining. Real networks require valid block data, consensus rules, targets and network participation.

Golden rule

Measure before making claims. CICAN separates benchmark measurements, live pool results, estimates and verified facts.
