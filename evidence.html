CICAN / EVIDENCE SYSTEM

Show the evidence.

CICAN separates what we directly measured from what we calculated, estimated or learned from an external source. This prevents a guess from becoming a “fact” simply because it appears on a website.

🟢 MEASURED

Directly observed

Captured from the actual device or experiment: hashrate, accepted shares, rejected shares, runtime, temperature or measured electrical input.

Example: Pixel 6 observed at 317–353 H/s during our live RandomX experiment.

🔵 CALCULATED

Derived from measurements

Numbers produced by applying a transparent formula to measured inputs.

Example: H/s per watt calculated from hashrate and measured input power.

🟡 ESTIMATED

Model or projection

Future-looking or uncertain numbers based on assumptions such as coin price, network conditions, expected yield or electricity cost.

Estimates can change. They are never presented as guaranteed earnings.

⚪ EXTERNAL SOURCE

Published elsewhere

Facts or documentation obtained from an external source. CICAN should link to the original or most authoritative source whenever practical.

Our evidence rule

  1. Record the raw observation.
  2. State the hardware and software configuration.
  3. State the measurement conditions.
  4. Separate calculations from observations.
  5. Label assumptions behind estimates.
  6. Link important external claims to their source.
  7. Never upgrade an estimate into a fact.

Why this matters

A mining number without its method is difficult to reproduce. CICAN's goal is not to publish the biggest number. It is to publish a number another person can understand, question and, where possible, reproduce.
